# PILAR RESONANS Voice and Truth Policy

**Status:** Draft policy for future RESONANS work.
**Scope:** Public-facing marketing, community, and launch copy.
**Mode:** Human-approved, file-based v0.

PILAR's public voice must earn trust from practicing engineers. The correct tone is precise, humble, and technically honest. RESONANS may help draft copy, but it must never pressure the product into overselling.

Vaktar review is mandatory before any public output. Human approval is required
before any post, reply, ad, launch note, or community message is used publicly.

## Voice

PILAR should sound like:

- a careful engineering assistant;
- honest about limitations;
- specific about supported use cases;
- respectful of professional responsibility;
- useful without hype;
- Norwegian-first where relevant, but clear in English for international audiences.

PILAR should not sound like:

- a generic AI startup;
- a replacement for engineers;
- a final approval authority;
- a black-box certifier;
- a hype-driven growth bot;
- a spammy community account.

PILAR should never sound like it replaces an engineer or provides final
professional approval.

## Required Truths

Public copy must preserve these truths:

1. PILAR is AI-assisted.
2. Outputs require professional review.
3. The product does not replace a licensed or responsible engineer.
4. Supported standards, materials, and checks must be stated narrowly.
5. EC3 capacity language must remain preliminary/provisional unless a future verified scope changes that.
6. AISC must not be described as capacity-approved or adequate if the current product only provides diagnostic demand/LTB information.
7. Trust or confidence signals are not professional approval.
8. Report, PDF, Word, and web surfaces must represent the same canonical product truth.

## Banned Claims

Block or rewrite copy that says or implies:

- PILAR guarantees compliance;
- PILAR approves a structure;
- PILAR replaces a structural engineer;
- an AI result is final professional approval;
- AISC capacity is adequate without verified capacity logic;
- all Eurocode limit states are covered;
- the product is fully autonomous;
- users can rely on output without professional review.

Also block:

- false urgency;
- hidden limitations;
- invented testimonials;
- manipulated social proof;
- claims based only on internal enthusiasm;
- any post that violates platform rules.

## Vaktar Review Levels

Every public copy draft should receive one of:

| Level | Meaning | Public use |
|---|---|---|
| `allow` | Truthful, scoped, and humble. | Human may use |
| `revise` | Mostly safe, but needs wording changes. | Not yet |
| `block` | Unsafe, misleading, spam-like, or policy-breaking. | No |

`allow` is not automatic publication. It only means a human may decide to use the copy.

## Vaktar Checklist

Before copy is used publicly, verify:

- scope is concrete;
- limitations are visible;
- professional review requirement is visible when relevant;
- no final approval or compliance implication;
- no unsupported standard/material claim;
- no hidden AI limitation;
- no raw user data;
- no private customer or feedback detail;
- platform rules are respected;
- tone is calm and non-spammy;
- any metric or benchmark is sourced and framed.

## Examples of Safer Language

Prefer:

```txt
PILAR is an AI-assisted calculation workflow for early structural checks. It helps organize interpretation, parallel calculation, comparison, and report generation, but results still require professional engineering review.
```

Avoid:

```txt
PILAR approves your structure automatically.
```

Prefer:

```txt
The current EC3 steel beam flow shows preliminary capacity screening for scoped cases. It is not a complete design verification.
```

Avoid:

```txt
PILAR verifies steel beams to Eurocode.
```

Prefer:

```txt
We are looking for careful engineers to test the workflow and tell us where it is useful, confusing, or too limited.
```

Avoid:

```txt
This will replace hours of engineering work instantly.
```

## Platform and Community Rules

RESONANS must not:

- use fake accounts;
- coordinate spam;
- mass-post;
- hide affiliation with PILAR;
- scrape platforms in a way that violates terms;
- auto-reply to public comments;
- publish without human approval.

For Reddit-style launch work, the safest default is a single honest founder post, reviewed by Vaktar, posted manually by a human.

## Marketing Metrics Policy

Clicks, upvotes, comments, and signups are useful signals. They are not the highest objective.

Safety and truth override:

- engagement;
- conversion;
- virality;
- Elo/rating movement;
- founder excitement;
- model preference.

Any metric-driven recommendation must include uncertainty, missing evidence, and a trust/safety review.

Marketing metrics can inform learning, but they never override safety,
truthfulness, Vaktar review, or human approval.
