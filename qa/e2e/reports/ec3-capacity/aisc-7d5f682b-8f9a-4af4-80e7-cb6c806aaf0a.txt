Step 4 — LTB Qualitative Screening
Lateral-torsional buckling (LTB) governs the available flexural strength when the unbraced length Lb exceeds the limiting unbraced length Lp (full plastic moment) and potentially Lr (inelastic-to-elastic LTB boundary). A quantitative assessment requires verified AISC Manual values for Lp, Lr, Zx, Sx, J, Cw, rts, and ho — none of which are available as verified input for this run.

Qualitative assessment:

1. The W12x26 is a relatively shallow (d ≈ 12 in) wide-flange section with a modest flange width. Shallow W-shapes tend to have lower torsional stiffness and shorter Lp and Lr limits compared to deeper, wider sections.

2. Lb = 20 ft represents a long unbraced length relative to the nominal depth of a W12 section. For typical W12 compact sections of ASTM A992 steel, the unbraced length of 20 ft is expected to be well beyond Lp and likely near or beyond Lr — placing the section in the inelastic or potentially elastic LTB range.

3. With Cb = 1.0 (conservative assumption — uniform moment), no amplification of the LTB capacity is taken. No refined Cb value is computed or assumed without verified input.

4. Conclusion of screening: LTB is assessed as LIKELY CRITICAL for this configuration. The available moment phib*Mn is expected to be meaningfully less than phib*Mp. A complete capacity check with verified AISC section properties is required before proceeding with design.

03.1 — ENGINEERING ASSESSMENT

Engineer A and Engineer B produced exact numerical agreement across all computed quantities, with no discrepancies identified by the Comparator — the match status is confirmed. The governing load combination wu = 1.2D + 1.6L = 1.820 kip/ft is standard ASCE 7 LRFD practice for gravity-only loading; the 1.4D alternate combination (0.630 kip/ft) does not govern and was correctly set aside. The midspan moment formula Mu = wu·L²/8 and support shear formula Vu = wu·L/2 are the appropriate closed-form expressions for a uniformly loaded simply supported beam, and Engineer B independently confirmed Mu via an equilibrium superposition route, which further validates the result. The qualitative LTB screening raises a significant concern: an unbraced length of Lb = 20 ft covering the full span is a long unbraced condition for a W12x26, a relatively shallow and lightly built wide-flange section, and LTB is assessed as likely critical — meaning the available moment strength phib·Mn is expected to be appreciably less than the full plastic moment capacity. This finding cannot be resolved quantitatively in this run because verified AISC Manual section properties (Zx, Sx, J, Cw, rts, ho, Lp, Lr) are not available as input, and the AISC data guard correctly prohibits generating those values from memory. Additionally, the AISC/ASCE standard profile is at an experimental support level in this application, which means factor values and code-compliance logic have not been independently validated by the platform — this is the basis for the approvedwithwarnings classification alongside the LTB criticality finding.
