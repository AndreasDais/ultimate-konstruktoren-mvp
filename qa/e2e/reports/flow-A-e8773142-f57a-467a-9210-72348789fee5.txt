03.1 — ENGINEERING ASSESSMENT

The load combination check confirms that 1.2D + 1.6L = 1.820 kip/ft governs over 1.4D = 0.630 kip/ft, as expected for a live-load-dominated case where the live load is nearly twice the dead load intensity. Both engineers applied standard simply supported beam mechanics — midspan moment formula wu × L² / 8 and support shear formula wu × L / 2 — and Engineer B performed an independent free-body cross-check at midspan, confirming Mu = 91.00 kip-ft by a second method. The complete numerical agreement (0% difference on all paired result fields) and the internal consistency checks give high confidence in the demand values. The critical open item is LTB: with Lb = 20 ft equal to the full span and no intermediate bracing, a W12x26 is exposed to a potentially large reduction in available flexural strength below the plastic moment capacity. Both engineers correctly applied Cb = 1.0 as a conservative bound for this screening; No refined Cb value is computed or assumed without verified input. The AISC/ASCE standard profile is experimental in this application, which is the basis for the approved-with-warnings status rather than a full approval; the load factors themselves (1.2 and 1.6 from ASCE 7) are well-established and are not the source of uncertainty.
